PriceTap — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026 · Developer: Digik Labs

1. Overview

PriceTap ("we", "our", or "the App") helps users compare grocery cart prices, pack weights, delivery fees, and credit-card offers across quick-commerce platforms (such as Swiggy Instamart, Blinkit, Zepto, Amazon Now, and Flipkart Minutes) and prepare their shopping cart on their chosen platform.

2. Data Processing & On-Device Execution

  • Local Session & Cart Data: Grocery lists, saved baskets, and store WebView sessions are stored locally on your device. PriceTap does not transmit or store your third-party shopping account credentials or payment details on any external server.
  • Location Information: If you provide a delivery pincode or approximate location, it is used solely on-device to fetch accurate local store pricing, stock availability, and delivery ETAs for your area.
  • Cart-Only Boundary: PriceTap only searches products and prepares carts on your device. You always review and complete checkout directly inside the merchant's official app or website.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

PriceTap displays banner advertisements via Google AdMob to keep the app free. Google AdMob may use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and coarse diagnostic data to serve relevant or non-personalized ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your saved baskets, card preferences, and comparison history are stored locally on your device (`SharedPreferences`), you can clear all app data at any time from inside the app or via your device's system settings.
